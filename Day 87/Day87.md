# 🚀 DAY 87 / 200 — NEURAL NETWORKS FUNDAMENTALS

## 🧠 Masterclass: Interview Q&A on Neural Networks

### Fundamentals
1. **What is an Artificial Neuron?**
   It's a mathematical function conceptually modeled after biological neurons. It calculates a weighted sum of its inputs, adds a bias, and passes it through an activation function.
2. **What is Forward Propagation?**
   The process of pushing data through the network layers sequentially to calculate the predicted output and measure it against the loss function.

### Deep Learning Mechanics
3. **What is Backpropagation?**
   The mechanism of calculating gradients by moving backward through the network, using the calculus chain rule, to determine how each weight should be updated to minimize the loss.
4. **Why do we need Activation Functions?**
   Without non-linear activations (like ReLU or Sigmoid), stacking layers would simply result in a giant linear transformation, completely defeating the purpose of a deep network's ability to model complex, curved boundaries.
